## About the Project

**RestroPulse** is a restaurant performance and analytics application that started from a real problem in my own restaurant business.

While running the restaurant, I realized that simply knowing the daily or monthly sales was not enough to understand how the business was actually performing. I did not have a clear view of important metrics such as profitability, expenses, food costs, wastage, average order value, or which menu items were contributing the most to the business.

RestroPulse was created to solve that problem.

The goal is to provide restaurant owners with a simple way to track and analyze the key numbers that matter in day-to-day operations, including sales, expenses, profit, order volume, average order value, food costs, wastage, and menu performance.

Rather than trying to become a complete POS or restaurant management system, RestroPulse focuses specifically on **business performance and decision-making**. It aims to help owners understand where the restaurant is making money, where costs are increasing, which menu items are performing well, and whether the business is improving over time.

### Core Features

- Track daily restaurant sales and order volume
- Monitor dine-in, takeaway, and delivery revenue
- Record and categorize business expenses
- Track food wastage and its financial impact
- Calculate average order value and profitability
- Analyze individual menu item performance
- Identify best-selling and most profitable items
- Monitor food cost percentages and margins
- View daily, weekly, and monthly performance trends
- Compare current performance with previous periods
- Provide a simple dashboard representing the restaurant's overall **pulse**

RestroPulse is being developed with a **mobile-first approach**, making it practical for small and medium restaurant owners who primarily manage their businesses through smartphones.

The initial version is built using **Flutter** with **Supabase** as the backend, keeping the project simple, fast to develop, and easy to improve based on real-world usage in my own restaurant.

The long-term vision is for RestroPulse to become a lightweight decision-support tool that helps restaurant owners move beyond simply knowing their revenue and instead understand whether their restaurant is actually **healthy, profitable, and improving**.
